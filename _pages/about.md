<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>

I'm Fengjie (Frankie) Lu, an audio researcher at **Humanify AI**. I did both my B.S. and M.S. at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn), where my master's was in AI at the College of Computer Science and Technology.

My research focuses on **large audio-language models for audio and speech understanding and retrieval**. That includes universal audio embeddings that can be steered by natural-language instructions, speaker-aware ("who said what") speech retrieval, and compact, structured audio tokenization.
